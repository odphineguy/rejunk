# Arizona Disposal Facilities Research Report

## Republic Services - Germann Transfer Station
- **Address:** 11530 E. Germann Rd., Chandler, AZ 85286
- **Website:** [republicservices.com](https://www.republicservices.com/locations/arizona/chandler-trash-pickup-and-recycling)
- **Phone:** 480-222-8434
- **Hours:** Monday - Friday 6:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Verified via Yahoo/Yelp; Chandler City site mentions 6 AM - 2 PM for its own RSWCC facility nearby, but Republic's gate hours for commercial are typically longer).
- **Pricing:** 
  - **MSW/C&D:** ~$70/ton (Based on Chandler ratepayer fee for 400lb+; commercial rates often mirror or slightly exceed this).
  - **Minimum Charge:** Likely ~$14 - $20 (Based on small load flat rates in the area).
- **Acceptance:**
  - **Tires:** Accepted (Separated, fees apply).
  - **Appliances:** Accepted (Washers, dryers, stoves, etc.). Refrigerant units (fridges/AC) ~$20 fee.
  - **Hazardous Waste:** No (Household Hazardous Waste by appointment only at City facilities).
- **Recycling:** Yes, accepts cardboard, metals, etc. Price/ton varies; often no charge for clean recyclables if separated.

## Weinberger Waste Disposal - Cooper Transfer Station
- **Address:** 619 N. Cooper Road, Gilbert, AZ 85233
- **Website:** [gwwd.com](https://www.gwwd.com/transfer-stations)
- **Phone:** 480-372-2459 (Direct) / 602-278-9155 (Customer Service)
- **Hours:** Monday - Friday 5:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Saturday 5:00 AM - 2:00 PM, Sunday Closed.
- **Pricing:** 
  - **MSW/C&D/Green Waste:** Gate rates typically ~$50 - $60/ton for commercial haulers.
  - **Minimum Charge:** Typically 1 ton or a flat "small load" fee (~$40).
- **Acceptance:**
  - **Tires:** NO.
  - **Appliances:** NO (Unacceptable waste list includes batteries/hazardous; appliances usually not listed as acceptable).
  - **Hazardous Waste:** NO.
- **Recycling:** Yes, diverts thousands of tons; accepts green waste and inert materials for processing.
