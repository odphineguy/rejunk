# Haul or Call Workflow

## Trigger Conditions

Drivers use Haul or Call when service cannot proceed normally, including locked gates, blocked access, customer not home, unable to locate, customer not ready, unsafe condition, scope dispute, disposal access problem, or another service blocker.

## Driver Responsibilities

- Report the blocker from the assigned job.
- Add notes and photos when useful.
- Call dispatch and confirm the call in the app.
- Remain at the location until dispatch sends instructions or records a release.
- Follow dispatch’s documented resolution.

There is no fixed wait timer. Elapsed time is awareness-only.

## Dispatch Responsibilities

- Review the exception queue.
- Contact the customer when needed.
- Record contact attempts and results.
- Send operational instructions to the driver.
- Authorize proceed, wait, return later, reschedule, skip stop, unable to service, cancel job, or another resolution.
- Record release before the driver leaves a blocked location when departure is allowed.

## Restrictions

- Drivers cannot cancel jobs.
- Drivers cannot skip stops.
- Drivers cannot resolve their own blocker issues.
- Drivers cannot release themselves from a location.
- Drivers cannot approve added scope or see approved dollar amounts.

## Required Activity Records

Activity history must record blocker report, driver call confirmation, customer contact attempts, dispatch instructions, resolution type, release, skip/cancel/reschedule decisions, and added-scope decisions.
