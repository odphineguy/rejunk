## Design Context

### Users
Phoenix metro and East Valley homeowners, renters, families, and property managers who need junk removed, a local move handled, or furniture assembled. They often arrive on a phone and want a trustworthy crew, a clear price, and a fast online way to take the next step.

### Brand Personality
Capable, local, straightforward. The experience should feel calm, established, and hands-on—not playful, corporate, or overproduced.

### Aesthetic Direction
A clean editorial service-business site built around strong real-world crew photography, deep green neutrals, generous spacing, and restrained bright-green actions. Premium means clear hierarchy and careful execution, not decorative labels, generic cards, or “AI slop.”

### Design Principles
- Give each section one clear job and each decision point one primary action.
- Prefer online booking and service-specific forms; keep the phone number as footer contact information.
- Let useful headlines and real job photography carry the hierarchy; remove redundant eyebrow labels and ornamental tags.
- Keep service promises specific, plainspoken, and supported by real reviews and credentials.
- Preserve accessible, responsive behavior and make the fastest mobile path obvious.
